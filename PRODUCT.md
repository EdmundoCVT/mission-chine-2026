# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: buildless HTML, CSS and JavaScript, chosen for reliable offline-capable local storage, low maintenance, and static Cloudflare-compatible hosting.

## Users

- Primary: Edmond Tavares, preparing and running a commercial mission from an iPhone and computer.
- Presentation audience: Edmond's uncle, who needs a concise, professional view of the plan, budget, risks and recommended decisions.

## Product Purpose

Provide one visual operating center for the 23 November–6 December 2026 Guangzhou mission: test demand, qualify suppliers, control the budget, track orders and repayment, and evaluate a concrete opportunity in Sal.

## Positioning

The product connects one commercial loop—China sourcing, France sales, and Sal tourism opportunities—in a single mission ledger rather than splitting travel notes, supplier data and financial follow-up across separate tools.

## Operating Context

Used before, during and after the trip. Records may be entered from a phone in markets or supplier meetings. The uncle-facing presentation must exclude personal notes and unpresentable hypotheses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two modes: Pilotage and Présentation.
- Editable local data, formulas, search, filters, photo attachments, CSV/JSON exports and print view.
- No authentication in V1 and no sensitive, banking, identity, password or confidential client data.
- All illustrative commercial records are labelled “exemple provisoire”; unconfirmed Sal opportunities remain hypotheses.
- Architecture leaves room for later protected storage and AI assistance, but V1 is local-first.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Mission Chine 2026. Route line: Chine → France → Sal. Voice: energetic and operational in Pilotage; sober and decision-oriented in Présentation.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed facts are limited to Edmond's identity, trip dates, Guangzhou as the principal destination, the €3,000 available envelope, the requested route and objectives. No supplier, price, customer demand or revenue is verified; the interface must not imply otherwise.

## Product Principles

- Make the next decision visible.
- Separate verified facts, estimates, hypotheses and provisional examples.
- Keep calculations transparent and editable.
- Work comfortably on iPhone and desktop.
- Preserve exports so the project can move to protected storage later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, at least 16px body text, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support and responsive tables.

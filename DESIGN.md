# Design system — Mission Chine 2026

The interface is an expedition ledger crossed with an international route board: disciplined navy surfaces, warm paper, decisive vermilion and a turquoise Sal accent. Pilotage feels active and dense; Présentation removes operational notes and shifts to a calmer, board-ready rhythm.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#102A43`; deep surface: `#081D2B`; paper: `#F7F1E6`; white: `#FFFFFF`.
- Action red: `#E8442E`; route blue: `#167D9A`; Sal teal: `#0F9D8A`; warning amber: `#D88B24`.
- Type: system sans for durable loading and tabular numerals; bold condensed-looking headings created through weight and measure, never decorative effects.
- Radius: 14px panels, 999px only for compact status controls.
- Elevation: one soft offset shadow, no border-plus-shadow duplication.

## Composition

The first viewport behaves as a mission brief: route, dates, objective and live readiness are one integrated scene over an authored decorative route image. Operational pages favor ledgers, split panels and strong section landmarks rather than grids of interchangeable cards.

## Interaction

One route-line animation on load; all other movement is restrained. Side navigation becomes a horizontal mobile dock. Editing happens inline in dedicated forms, with autosave feedback and reversible local reset.

## Modes

- Pilotage: navy navigation, energetic red/teal signals, editable controls and operational notes.
- Présentation: paper-led palette, reduced controls, curated decision content, print-ready layout.
